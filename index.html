Understanding the Himalayans

The Himalayas are one of the world's most remarkable mountain ranges, stretching across Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan, and Afghanistan. They are home to Mount Everest, the world's highest mountain, as well as diverse landscapes, wildlife, and communities. For millions of people, the Himalayas are more than mountains. They are home to unique cultures, traditions, languages, and ways of life shaped by the mountain environment. Explore the Himalayas through its people, stories, culture, and landscapes.

How it works

From a local memory to a mapped public story

Understanding the Himalayans brings together place, experience and community knowledge in one accessible archive.

01

Share

People can submit a story with their name, verified email, location, coordinates, photos and the experience they want to preserve.

02

Review

Submitted stories stay pending until the project team checks the information and decides whether to publish them.

03

Explore

Approved stories become part of the public archive, connected to a map and a Google Maps location for easier exploration.

What we document

More than a disaster map

Each record can preserve what happened, where it happened, when it happened, how people were affected, what communities remember, and what lessons or local knowledge should not be lost. The aim is to keep the human story alongside the geographic record.

A

People

Firsthand accounts, community memories and lived experiences.

B

Place

Province, district, locality and precise coordinates where available.

C

Evidence

Photos, notes, links and contextual information that help explain each story.
